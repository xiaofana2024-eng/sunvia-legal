Sunvia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Sunvia is a local photography session and exposure logging app. This policy explains what data the app handles on your device.

Data we store on device

  • Session aliases, direction notes, shutter/aperture/ISO values, text notes, and lifecycle state.
  • Optional photos you capture or select for a session or profile avatar.
  • Optional speech-to-text results that you generate into notes.
  • Local purchase records for gift packs unlocked via Apple In-App Purchase.

Permissions

  • Camera / Microphone / Photos / Speech Recognition: requested only when you use the related feature.
  • App Tracking Transparency / Notifications: system prompts may appear once when you first open the home tab. Sunvia does not use tracking identifiers for cross-app advertising and does not send push or local notification campaigns.

Network and third parties

Core logging works offline. In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. Opening Privacy Policy or Terms uses Safari to load these pages. We do not operate a Sunvia account backend for this version.

Retention and deletion

Data remains in the app sandbox until you clear sessions, delete media, or remove the app. Uninstalling Sunvia removes local app data.

Children

Sunvia is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about this demo build, use the in-app Support entry once a production support channel is configured by the publisher.
